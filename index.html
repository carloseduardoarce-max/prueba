1. History of the Web

    Today, the internet is part of our daily lives. We use it for many things, such as studying, watching videos, listening to music and talking with other people. However, the Web was very different when it first started.

    The World Wide Web was created by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989. He was a British scientist working at CERN in Switzerland. He wanted to find a better way for scientists to share information between computers. At that time, sharing documents was not very easy.

    In 1990, he created the first web browser and web server. He also created the first website. He developed three important technologies called HTML, HTTP and URLs. These technologies are still used on websites today.

    In 1993, CERN made the Web free for everyone. This was very important because more people could use it and create their own websites.

    At the beginning, websites were very simple. They mainly had text and some links. There were not many pictures, colours or videos. Today, websites look very different. We can watch videos, buy products, study online and communicate with people from different countries, i think the Web has changed a lot since it started. It has made many things easier, especially communication and access to information. It is now something that many people use every day.
